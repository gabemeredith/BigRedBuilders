What is this?

A head-to-head ranking of the most cracked software engineers and founders in the Ivy League.

Two Ivy profiles show up. Names hidden. You pick which one is more impressive on paper.

Every vote updates each person's Elo rating. Climb the leaderboard. Land on the tier list.

It's gimmicky and unserious. Everyone on here is extremely talented and extremely tired.

Who can be on here?
Ivy undergrads + alumni within 2 years. SWE or founder track. Self-submitted or nominated.
How do I get added?
Submit at rivyalry.com/submit. We verify via .edu email.
How do I get off?
One email. No questions. Removed within 24 hours.
Is this fair?
No. It's a popularity contest with stats. That's the whole bit.
